Research

Introduction

My research focuses on VLSI and digital integrated circuit design for emerging technologies. I investigate circuit approaches to energy-efficient computing and AI/ML accelerator arithmetic, with current projects in superconducting logic.

My work spans schematic design, functional simulation, custom physical layout, physical verification, parasitic extraction, and post-layout characterization. I evaluate timing, power dissipation, device count, and operating margins to guide circuit optimization.

Asynchronous Digital Logic

I develop dynamic single flux quantum (DSFQ) circuits that use self-resetting loops to perform logic operations without a distributed clock.

  • Asynchronous XOR and half-adder circuits: Circuit design, transient simulation, timing characterization, and operating-margin optimization.
  • Half-adder test chip: Custom physical layout in KLayout, on-chip test structures, DRC/LVS verification, InductEx parasitic extraction, and post-layout verification. Submitted to MIT Lincoln Laboratory for fabrication.

Arithmetic Circuits for AI/ML Accelerators

I investigate bipolar unary SFQ multiplication for accelerator arithmetic, including pulse encoding, circuit behavior, and multiplication accuracy.

  • Bipolar unary multiplier: Developed a circuit with 22 Josephson junctions, an 18.5% reduction relative to the 27-junction reference design.
  • Physical design and characterization: Multiplier layout using the AIST QuFab PHSTP PDK, with parameter-margin, timing, and power analysis.

VLSI Design and Physical Verification

My work combines circuit simulation, parameter sweeps, custom IC layout, physical verification, parasitic extraction, and post-layout simulation. Tools include Cadence Virtuoso, KLayout, InductEx, JSIM, WRSpice, Python, and MATLAB.
